Mission and Vision

Purpose of Establishment

To provide debt settlement services to individual debtor(s) and to support the financial recovery of people with low income.

To cultivate a culture of proper credit management through counseling and education on individual credit management and to contribute to the stable lives of people and to well-balanced economic and social development.

Mission

We support credit rebuilding and economic rehabilitation of individual debtors so that we contribute to the stable lives of people with low to moderate income

Vision

"Comprehensive Credit Counseling Hub" that supports debt relief as well as economic independence
