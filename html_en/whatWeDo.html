Our Core Services

Debt relief programs
  • We examine your credit and debt problems and suggest an appropriate solution.
  • As for over-indebtedness, we provide debt relief programs and help you rebuild your credit.
Assistance in Applying for Individual Rehabilitation and Bankruptcy
  • We provide free assistance in filing for individual rehabilitation and bankruptcy to over-indebted people who need judicial relief.
  • Our Fast-Track Agreement with major courts across the country enables a simplified and speedy filing process.
Micro Credit
  • We offer small loans to low-income and poor-credit individuals who have difficulties in getting services from financial institutions.
  • We jointly operate the Financial Inclusion Service Center with the Korea Inclusive Finance Agency which is founded on the basis of Microfinance Support Act」
Credit education
  • We offer teenagers, college students and general public education about financial management, reasonable consumption, and the methods of credit management.
  • We develop various campaigns with the government and other public organizations in order to build a healthy credit environment.
Credit and Welfare Consulting
  • We help our DMP(debt management plan) clients achieve stable control over their finances and gain access to financial health.
