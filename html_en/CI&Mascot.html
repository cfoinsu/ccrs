CI & Mascot

CI

Symbol mark
The orange color of our symbol represents warmth and stability. Its form depicts an individual who has regained hope through our support, reflecting our belief that personal credit recovery helps build a healthier credit society and supports national creditworthiness.
Logo type
The font of our logo features rounded elements that convey a warm and stable atmosphere. Its softly curved shape also reflects a sense of friendliness.
Signature

Mascot

Saeromi
Saeromi, the mascot of the Credit Counseling & Recovery Service (CCRS), is named after a Korean word meaning “to begin anew.”
Inspired by a roly-poly toy, Saeromi represents our belief that everyone can rise again with the right support.
