Global Partnership

The Credit Counseling and Recovery Service (CCRS) works closely with overseas organizations to advance financial inclusion and strengthen support systems for individuals facing debt-related difficulties.

Through knowledge-sharing, joint research, and capacity-building initiatives, CCRS contributes to the development of effective household debt-relief frameworks worldwide.

Key Areas of Collaboration

  • Exchange of best practices in credit counseling and household debt-relief programs
  • Joint research on financial inclusion and consumer protection
  • Capacity-building for financial counseling institutions in emerging markets
  • Participation in global forums and international working groups

Main Activities

  • Hosting and participating in international conferences and seminars
  • Sharing expertise on CCRS household debt-relief programs and Micro Credit
  • Providing training programs for partner institutions
  • Building cooperative networks to better protect financially vulnerable individuals

CCRS continues to expand its global partnerships to help financially vulnerable individuals achieve sustainable financial recovery and to promote responsible financial ecosystems around the world.

Global Credit Counseling Alliance

In August 2024, CCRS entered into Memorandums of Understanding (MOUs) with leading credit-counseling organizations in the United States, the United Kingdom, and Canada. Together, we launched the Global Credit Counseling Alliance (GCCA) to establish a framework for mutual cooperation and information sharing.

Through this partnership, CCRS expects to deepen collaboration in key areas such as :

  • Case studies on debtor support
  • Household debt-relief policy and system improvements
  • Innovation in credit-counseling services

CCRS will continue to expand global partnerships to strengthen financial inclusion, promote safe credit practices, and support sustainable financial recovery among vulnerable individuals.
