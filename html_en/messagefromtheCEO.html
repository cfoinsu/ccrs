Message from the CEO

Welcome to the Credit Coundeling & Recovery Service homepage.

We will help you overcome
hardship and complete
rehabilitation for your
better life.


As the credit counseling organization in Korea, we help those who are suffering from over-indebtedness and credit problems re-establish credit and improve their financial health.


By one-on-one counseling service, we diagnose your financial situation and provide tailored solution such as interest rate reduction, reduction and discharge of debt and repayment deferral for those who have difficulties in dealing with their debt problems. Furthermore, in cooperation with courts, we also help you make application for court’s debt felief plan or bankruptcy discharge.
In addition, we provide microfinace service for those who are steadily making payments via “debt adjustment plan” and play a great role in financial educations for various groups to establish a “desirable consumer credit habits” in korea.

Also, we provide holistic credit-welfare consulting service and credit score improvement program, so that we can support people to improve their financial lives.

We are aiming to your financial advocates, working with the spirit of service and professional virtues. We will help you overcome hardship and complete rehabilitation for your better life.
