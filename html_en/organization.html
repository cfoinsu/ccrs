Commission

The commission, composed of 15 members including the chairman, is the highest decision-making body of our organization. Its members are appointed by the Financial Services Commission among the following individuals : the Chairman of the Korea Federation of Banks, the Chairman of the Korea Life Insurance Association, the Chairman of the General Insurance Association of Korea, the Chairman of the Credit Finance Association of Korea, the President of the Korea Federation of Savings Banks, the CEO of Mutual Finance at the National Agricultural Cooperative Federation, the Vice-President of the Korea Inclusive Finance Agency, and other individuals who have rich knowledge and experience in the area of consumer protection and Household debt relief programs.
