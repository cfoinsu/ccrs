Credit and Welfare Counseling

promises to boost credit scores and improve quality of life for our DMP (Debt Management Plan) clients.

What is Credit and Welfare Consulting?

Credit Counseling and Recovery Service (CCRS) offers Credit and Welfare Consulting to encourage DMP clients to quickly stabilize their credit status.
The service provides tailored information based on your payment status. It consists of Credit Consulting, Credit Helper, and Credit Vitamin.

Benefits of Credit and Welfare Consulting

Your credit scores can rise faster

If you follow the solution's rules, your credit scores will rise steadily

You can use various programs from our partner organizations

You can achieve stronger financial fitness. After using Credit and Welfare Consulting

Services in Credit and Welfare Consulting

Credit Consulting

one-to-one telephone counseling service by professional consultants

  • Sign up to the service via our mobile application
  • Those who qualify will receive a text message containing signup information link
  • We offer one-to-one telephone counseling with professional consultants
  • The consulting introduces credit management methods, various financial support programs and customized welfare programs

Credit Helper

Credit management service on mobile application

  • Sign up to the service via our mobile application
  • First session is available right after your signup. (Five sessions in total)
  • Check your credit scores
  • Test your credit management skills and credit status index
  • Get a customized solution to raise your credit scores
  • We offer one-to-one advice to improve your credit

Credit Vitamin

Useful information on credit management

Three sessions are available via kakaotalk notice, after your DMP is finalized

We provide FAQs such as unfreezing bank accounts and using debit cards via kakaotalk notice
