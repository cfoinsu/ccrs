The Debt relief system is a system that changes the repayment terms to help those who have difficulty repaying their debt due to unmanageable debt to get back on track financially.

The debt relief system is a system that supports those who have difficulty repaying their debts normally due to unmanageable debt by changing their repayment terms through methods such as extending the repayment period, repayment in installments, adjusting the interest rate, deferring repayment, and reducing debt, so that they can recover financially.

Analysis of Credit and Debt Situation

  • Analysis of your credit ratings and grades
  • Analysis of your transactions including debt and the use of credit cards
  • consulting to improve your financial structure by analyzing loan interest rates and the repayment schedule

Guide to Various Types of Debts Solutions

  • Before-overdue debt management, Pre-workout and Individual workout by the CCRS
  • Individual Rehabilitation and Bankruptcy

Guide to Financial Inclusion Services

  • Conversion of a high interest rate loan with lower rate
  • Microfinance for those who have low income or poor credit
  • Self-supporting programs integrating employment and welfare

Issuing Credit Counseling Reports

  • Presenting a general report on your credit status
  • Suggestions to improve your credit status or to prevent it from getting worse, considering tour credit rank, income, and assets

We are partners with Korea Inclusive Finance Agency which is founded on the basis of Micro Credit Support Act.
We jointly operate Financial Inclusion Service Centers in major cities across the country and offer one-stop financial services for common people.

  • Microfinance
  • We offer microfinance products such as Smile-microbank, Sunshine Loan, Dream Loan, and Security Deposit Loan.
  • Providing Debt Solution
  • Based on comprehensive assessment of your situation, we provide tailored debt solutions from Before-overdue debt management, Pre-workout and Individual workout by the CCRS to assistance in filing for individual rehabilitation or bankruptcy.
  • Integrating Employment and Welfare
  • Our links with the local government. the Ministry of Employment and the Ministry of Welfare enable us to offer employment and welfare counseling services.
  • General Counseling
  • We analyze your credit and financial status and provide comprehensive advice on your retirement plan, asset management, start-up business, etc.
