Article 1 (Purpose)
The purpose of this Act is to contribute to stabilization of ordinary people’s lives and balanced development of economy and society by supporting the financial life of ordinary people and the debt settlement for individual debtor(s) by establishing the Korea Inclusive Finance Agency and the Credit Counseling and Recovery Service.
