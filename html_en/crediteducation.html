Credit Education

Credit Education by CCRS

CCRS provides customized credit-education programs both online and offline for individuals and groups, including users of debt-relief programs and financially vulnerable individuals.

Education Program Overview

We offer education to help participants better understand personal finance, including :

  • Healthy spending habits
  • Responsible financial behavior
  • Importance of credit
  • Personal financial planning
  • Prevention of financial fraud
  • Debt-relief programs
  • Public welfare services

The curriculum is tailored to the participant’s age and financial circumstances.

Key Program Topics

Youth / University Students

  • Spending and allowance management
  • Basic understanding of credit
  • How to build and manage credit

Military Service Members

  • Saving and spending wisely
  • Credit-management skills
  • Debt-relief options available to service members

Adults / General Public

  • Credit-score management
  • Responsible credit-card use
  • Debt-management strategies
  • Latest financial information
  • Information on related support services

Student-loan / Micro Credit Users

  • Korea Student Aid Foundation programs
  • Interest-rate or late-fee benefits available upon course completion

Individual Rehabilitation / Bankruptcy Users

Mandatory courses provided in cooperation with 15 courts nationwide
CCRS is certified by the Ministry of Education as an Excellent Institution for Education Donation.

Online Credit education

We offer online lessons for a range of groups-from elementary, middle, and high-school students to the general public.
For online credit-education resources, please visit : www.educredit.or.kr

Talent Donation Activities

Participation in Education Donation Fair (KFASC)

We take part in the Education Donation Fair hosted by the Ministry of Education (organized by KFASC) to help students learn about the importance of credit and credit management.

Training Credit-Education Instructors

We improve the quality of our programs by offering workshops and training for credit-education instructors certified by the Financial Supervisory Service.

“One Instructor per School” Partnerships

Local CCRS offices partner with nearby schools to provide credit-education programs.

Ministry of Education Certification

In 2015, CCRS received the Ministry of Education’s Excellence in Education Donation certification for its contribution to nationwide financial-education activities.

Kyunghyang Financial Education Awards

CCRS co-hosts the annual Kyunghyang Financial Education Awards with the Financial Supervisory Service and The Kyunghyang Shinmun, recognizing organizations and individuals who contribute to improving financial education and raising public awareness of credit.
